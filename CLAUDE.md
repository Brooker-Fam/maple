# Maple

Maple's Log is a private, collaborative newborn tracker. Split out of [Brooker-Fam/brooker-wedding](https://github.com/Brooker-Fam/brooker-wedding), where it lived at `brooker.family/maple`. Full design and ops notes: `docs/maple.md`.

**This repo is public.** Never commit secrets, `.env*` files, real log entries, screenshots of the signed-in app, or personal family or health details. Use generic examples in shared app copy and documentation.

## Stack

- **Next.js 16** (App Router), TypeScript, **Tailwind CSS v4**, Framer Motion
- **Neon Postgres** (`@neondatabase/serverless`), **Vercel Blob**, Web Push (`web-push`), MCP (`mcp-handler`)
- **pnpm** 11, Node 22+
- **Vercel** project `maple` in the `guava-tri` team. Pushing to `main` deploys production.


## Hosting, origin and env

- The public origin comes from `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPLE_ORIGIN` (default `https://maplebaby.app`) via `src/lib/maple/origin.ts`. Use `MAPLE_ORIGIN` / `MAPLE_HOST` / `MCP_URL` from there; never hard-code a host.
- Production env (Vercel): `DATABASE_URL`, `DATABASE_URL_UNPOOLED`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, `ADMIN_USER`, `ADMIN_PASS`, `CRON_SECRET`, `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` (the connected Blob store), `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPLE_ORIGIN`. Optional: `MCP_SECRET`, `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`/`VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `QSTASH_*`, `MAPLE_TICK_NET_MIN`, `MAPLE_ALLOWED_EMAILS`, `MAPLE_BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_KEY`.
- Additional sign-in env: email uses `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_EMAIL_DOMAIN` and the session key; optional `MAPLE_EMAIL_AUTH_DAILY_MAX` defaults to 1,000. Apple uses `APPLE_CLIENT_ID` (Services ID), `APPLE_TEAM_ID`, `APPLE_KEY_ID`, `APPLE_PRIVATE_KEY` (P-256 `.p8` PEM). New methods stay hidden until configured. Apple setup, callback and relay registration: `docs/auth.md`.
- Email (Resend, all environments): `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_EMAIL_DOMAIN` (`maplebaby.app`). Addressed invites send through `emailInvite` after the sender's daily cap; a newly created family's owner gets `emailWelcome`. Without the env vars, `sendEmail` (`src/lib/email/`) returns not-sent and invite callers show the link instead. Invite links expire after 7 days, but using or expiring one does not erase its address; revoking or replacing an unused invite deletes that row. See `docs/maple.md` ("Email").
- Session cookies, MCP tokens and VAPID keys are derived from `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` / `ADMIN_PASS` (or `MCP_SECRET`), which match brooker-wedding's. Maple-only `MAPLE_SESSION_SECRET` (with `MAPLE_SESSION_SECRET_PREV` holding the old `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) and `MCP_SECRET` (with `MCP_SECRET_PREV` holding whatever signed before) replace them; what only a `_PREV` key opens reaches family 1 only. Sign-up (`MAPLE_SIGNUP`) stays off until both are set. Pin `VAPID_*` before changing `MCP_SECRET`. Order: `docs/multi-family-plan.md` Phase 0.

## Database location (read before any schema change)

Maple uses its own Neon database. The original `maple_*` tables remain intact in brooker-wedding's database, protected by statement triggers that reject writes. Never remove those blockers or roll back to a deployment using the old database without reconciling writes made since the transfer. `upload_rate_limit` was excluded from the transfer; each app has its own table. Cutover and rollback details: `docs/multi-family-plan.md` 2.6.

`MAPLE_READ_ONLY=1` pauses every Maple API, OAuth, and waitlist request with a retryable 503; changing it requires a deployment. Complete preparation and verification of the transfer utility before enabling it. Keep the pause as short as possible because timer controls cannot queue offline.

- Migrations (`src/lib/migrate.ts`) stay additive and idempotent: `CREATE ... IF NOT EXISTS`, `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`. Never drop, rename or retype a column, and never add a `NOT NULL` column without a default: a Vercel instant rollback puts older code back on the migrated schema without a build. The contract steps are a separate script (plan Phase 3), never `migrate.ts`.
- Sends are claimed per phone in `maple_push_sent`, so a notice goes out once whichever deployment ticks. A recent legacy claim without a family keeps sign-up off (`olderCopyActive`). Its timestamp is not a reliable writer timestamp: wake claims use scheduled delivery times, and retries can refresh existing rows. Attribute copied claims to family 1 only after verifying the isolated database and the old database’s durable write blockers.
- All "today"/clock logic goes through `src/lib/maple/time.ts`, using each family's saved timezone (legacy default `America/New_York`), never the server's zone.

## The public site

`/` (the home page), `/privacy`, `/terms` and `/waitlist` live in `src/app/(site)/`, with parts in `src/components/site/`, drawn in the app's `mp-*` tokens (`site.css`). The header and footer link to the app as "Family sign-in"; `/maple*` is `noindex` (meta and `X-Robots-Tag`) and stays crawlable so that can be read.

- **Waitlist:** `POST /api/waitlist` (`src/lib/waitlist.ts`, table `maple_waitlist`) keeps an email address and nothing else. It has its own hourly bucket per connection (`checkWaitlistRateLimit`), a daily cap on new addresses (`WAITLIST_DAILY_MAX`), refuses cross-site posts (`Sec-Fetch-Site`), answers 204 whether or not the address was already there, and sends a form posted before the script loaded to `/waitlist?r=<outcome>`. `pnpm check:site`.
- **Legal copy follows the code.** Any change to what Maple keeps, sends, shows on a lock screen or deletes needs the matching change to `/privacy` or `/terms`, and a new `LEGAL_VERSION` / `LEGAL_UPDATED` in `src/components/site/legal.tsx`. `CONTACT_EMAIL` there is the only deletion and waitlist-removal route, so it must reach a real inbox.

## The app

Private, collaborative newborn tracker: nursing L/R timers, bottles, pumping, diapers + stool colors, sleep, tummy time, temperature/vitals/skin checks, growth on WHO percentiles (the girls' or boys' standard by the baby's sex, the measurements alone when it isn't set), notes and doctor questions, photos and milestones. Full design and ops notes: `docs/maple.md`.

- **Names and milestones:** Every family, including family 1, uses its saved baby name/nickname, sex-derived pronouns and care-person name in the app, Siri, MCP and chat. With no profile, copy falls back to "the baby" and "Mom". All families get the same neutral milestone presets. Older personal milestone ids have neutral display labels for compatibility, but are never offered for new moments. This copy cleanup does not rewrite saved entries, profile values or care-person keys.

- **Access:** Google, email codes and Apple verify identity; `maple_members` grants access (roles owner / member / viewer / photos), plus each person's level on her page (`care_access`: none / view / log, set by an owner apart from the role; `src/lib/maple/access.ts`, enforced only through `careAccess`; NULL is the role's default). Owners manage people in Settings → People & permissions, from the chat, and over MCP (`people-mcp.ts` over `people.ts`: `list_people`, and for owners `invite_person`, `change_access`, `remove_person`, `cancel_invite`); over MCP the three changes need `confirmed: true` after a read-back, and in the chat each waits for a tap on its card. Older releases ignore `care_access` and give every member all of her page, so roll forward: before any rollback past it, make restricted members viewers (`UPDATE maple_members SET role = 'viewer' WHERE role = 'member' AND care_access IS NOT NULL`), which older releases read as no page and no logging. `maple_auth_identities` maps verified email addresses and Apple subjects to the existing member key, preserving family access, removals and log attribution. Family settings explicitly connects another method; never merge separate accounts/logs automatically. First Google sign-in claims owner unless `MAPLE_ALLOWED_EMAILS` is set. Fallback sign-in with `ADMIN_USER`/`ADMIN_PASS` as the "admin" member. One-time invite links remain available. Outside family 1, adding someone by email is a pending invite (`maple_invites.email`): they join only by its link, signed in as that address, and a membership nobody accepted (`accepted_at`) opens nothing. Google redirects to `{site}/api/maple/auth/callback`, where `site` is the origin the browser is on (`signInSite`: this app, or `https://brooker.family`, which forwards here); each must be an authorized redirect URI on the OAuth client (`GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`). Apple uses `/api/maple/auth/apple/callback` with its own state/nonce validation. Email challenges expire in ten minutes, are single use and store HMACs plus encrypted context. Setup and checks: `docs/auth.md`.
- **Data:** `maple_entries` (one row per event: `kind` + JSONB `data`, metric units; running timers have `active = TRUE`; soft deletes; `client_id` for idempotent retries). All "today"/clock logic goes through `src/lib/maple/time.ts`, using each family's saved timezone (legacy default `America/New_York`), never the server's zone.
- **Medical guidance** lives in `src/lib/maple/guidelines.ts` (thresholds + exact copy) and `src/lib/maple/guidelines/development.ts` (sourced discoveries and milestones), sourced from AAP/CDC/WHO/ABM and the sources linked by each discovery. Alerts always say who to call; never suggest medicine doses. `insights.ts` applies the rules for both the app and the MCP tools. Warnings and reminders are off unless `settings.show_alerts` is true (Settings → On Today → Warnings & reminders; incomplete logging can make count-based alerts misleading), but readings are still checked as they're entered.
- **AI:** MCP server at `/api/maple/mcp` (Streamable HTTP, discrete tools in `src/lib/maple/mcp-tools.ts`). OAuth goes through the `/oauth/*` shim; connections (RFC 8707 `resource` = `{origin}/api/maple/mcp`) sign in with Google and get `maple:<email>` tokens, bound to that exact URL. Refresh tokens go only to codes redirected to Claude, ChatGPT, Muse's or Grok's exact callback, or loopback (`trustedRedirect`); admin ones lapse after 90 idle days. Setup page: `/maple/connect`.
- **In-app chat (the Chat tab):** Photos use the private, member-scoped `/api/maple/chat/images` upload/read route and Neon storage, separately from Moments. Up to eight images per message; original files are resized and stripped of metadata. Chat consent version 2 covers photos, and deleting history removes them.  `POST /api/maple/chat` runs the MCP tools for the signed-in member (`source: "chat"`, "via Maple chat") with `zai/glm-5.3-flash` through AI Gateway (OIDC; ZDR and no-training on every request), from the Chat tab: one continuous conversation per person, no New chat, nobody else's visible. The model reads only its recent end (`chat/window.ts`: whole exchanges, at most 24 messages and about 12K tokens); GET reads it a page at a time (`chat/history.ts`), DELETE removes all of it. Each member accepts short terms first (`maple_consents`, `consent.ts`; the POST refuses without them), and her page (medicines, temperatures, questions for her OB) reaches the chat only once she has turned that on herself (`chat_care`; until then her tools are out, Maple's tools leave her out, `ctx.care`, and a thread that ever ran with it open rolls over, `care_open`), and then only for owners and members whose level lets them read it (`chatCareOpen`, never a viewer: "view" brings her reads only; someone set to "none" opens only threads that never had it open, `threadAccess`). Requests route only to the price row's `providers` (ZDR, at or under its price). `delete_entry`, her `fix_mom_entry` delete and the people changes (`invite_person`, `change_access`, `remove_person`, owners only) wait for a tap (`undo_last` and edits, hers included, don't); viewers get reads only, decided on the server, and open only chats that ran as a viewer; her medicine-list tools are left out; the crisis help card shows even when a message is turned away. Caps fail closed; usage is token counts and micro-dollar BigInt cost only, never content. Optional env: `MAPLE_CHAT_MODEL` (must be priced in `chat/prices.ts`), `MAPLE_CHAT_DAILY_BUDGET_MICRO`, `MAPLE_CHAT_MONTHLY_BUDGET_MICRO` (family 1's), `MAPLE_CHAT_GLOBAL_MONTHLY_MICRO` (every other family together, default 25000000; `chatBudget`). An operator pauses one family's chat with `maple_families.settings.chat_paused = true`. `pnpm check:maple-chat`. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("In-app chat").
- **Hands-free:** `POST /api/maple/quick` with a personal token (Avatar → Settings → Siri & assistants) for iOS Shortcuts / Siri. Her `mom_dose` returns `next_ok_in_minutes` for a one-action Start Timer; "Maple status" includes her medicines for anyone who can read them. `back_and_forth` (with a feed running) goes both sides from now; `switch` while she's going both says there's nothing to switch.
- **Notifications:** Web Push, opt-in per phone (iPhone needs the Home Screen app): her medicines (OK again, set times, "Logged" that closes the loop on the other phones, never a dose or "take it now") and an opt-in nursing timer on the lock screen; nothing else notifies. `src/lib/maple/push.ts` sends (claimed once per phone, retried on failure, 3 h cutoff), `push-wake.ts` decides when (`dueMoments`) and is the clock: Upstash QStash wake-ups at the exact moments plus a safety-net schedule (`MAPLE_TICK_NET_MIN`, default 30), published from writes (`push-hooks.ts`, via `after()` in the stores, so the app, Siri and Claude all count) and scheduled ticks, never from polls. Without `QSTASH_*` env vars, `.github/workflows/maple-reminders.yml` (every 5 min, when GitHub runs it, and only once the repo variable `MAPLE_TICK_URL` is set) and open apps' polls drive `/api/maple/push/tick`; with them, the tick ignores GitHub runs away from :04/:34. The app sends a per-install `X-Maple-Device` id (`device.ts`) only so "Logged" skips the phone that did it; no person is ever named. The app's side is `src/components/maple/notify/`: one provider (mounted in `MapleApp`) owns this phone's state and the one Notifications sheet (`useNotifications().open()`, `NotificationsRow`), re-checks the subscription on each open (a dropped one reads "Stopped" with a one-tap Turn on), offers "Remind me at …" in a dose toast on a phone that's off (at most 3 times), and sign-out turns the phone off. VAPID keys derive from `MCP_SECRET` (else `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) unless `VAPID_*` are set; changing that secret makes every phone turn notifications on again. Family setup steps and copy: `docs/maple.md` → Notifications.
- **Tabs:** Today, the care person's page (labelled with their name; hidden for view-only guests), History (Timeline | Patterns behind one pinned switch), Moments, Chat (a full page for owners, members and viewers; mounted once visited, so its draft and a reply still coming survive a tab switch). Photos-only guests see Moments and basic account settings. Settings opens from the top-right avatar menu, which also holds Sign out. Settings groups Your account, This device, and Your family; it is not a bottom tab. A family still setting up (`onboarding/`) sees only its setup, as the Chat page in the Chat tab's frame (`chat/page.ts`). Which tab shows lives in `AppNav.tsx` (`useAppNav().go()`; "log"/"patterns" open History on that segment); `useCareNav()` opens her tab and the shared sheets: Help (`HelpSheet.tsx`, every "who do we call"), For the doctor (`DoctorSheet.tsx`: questions, visit summary, her doctor's numbers), her temperature, the picker and the Dose sheet. Today shows current status, three recent entries and compact logged totals. History → Patterns opens on Day clock (the dial and tallies); Compare days offers ribbons or circles with activity filters, Daily totals offers a table or charts and the diaper brigade, and Growth expands separately. More provides Log earlier and Bottle while nursing. Tabs and sheets participate in browser Back; explicit drafts prompt before dismissal. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("Where things live").
- **Dock:** Today's logging buttons (`Dock.tsx`, with her strip on top); every timer action lives there and in the Lantern, with `useTimerActions.tsx` saying what each tap did (Undo puts back exactly what the server had). Going back and forth (a `"B"` part: both sides, not tracked) is offered from a feed's second Switch, on a start within 30 minutes of the last feed, and in the card's … menu; then Switch's place only asks "Which side is she on now?" and saves nothing until answered. Settings → On Today has log-wide Bottles and Pumping switches (`settings.feeds_bottle` / `track_pump`, on unless switched off; `logSwitches` in `MoreSheet.tsx`): off hides the Bottle tile (Left and Right take its room) or Pump in More, never anything logged, and Claude and Siri can still log either. Feeds are counted from the log, never stored (`nursingRuns`, `feedClusters`, `lastFedAnchor`, `nextSideFor` in `feed.ts`, used by Today, History, Patterns, Claude and Siri alike): under 10 minutes off the breast is the same feed whether Pause or Done was tapped, 3+ feeds each starting under 30 minutes after the last ended are a cluster (each feed counts), and Last fed after a long back-and-forth feed counts from 40 minutes before it ended. The cluster copy and its sources live in `guidelines.ts`; there's no cluster alert. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("The nursing screen", "Medical guidance", "How it works").
- **Look:** its own calm, native-feeling skin, not the site's storybook one. Use the semantic `mp-*` colors (values in `src/app/maple/maple.css` cover day, dark and the night look, so no `dark:` variants), icons from `src/components/maple/icons.tsx`, and the kit in `ui.tsx`. No emoji in the app's chrome (members' avatar emoji and family-typed text are fine). Details in `docs/maple.md`.
- **Photos:** New uploads require the private Maple store via `MAPLE_BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`; uploads fail closed without it. Existing Maple photos have been migrated to the private store; `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` remains available for legacy compatibility and orphan cleanup. Photos are always served through the auth proxy `/api/maple/photo`, never by blob URL. The `photos` membership role opens only shared Moments and basic account controls. New invitations default to this role; owners can explicitly grant broader access. Photo edits retain stable photo IDs and optimistic version checks. The photo proxy revalidates access, and Share/Save exports authenticated bytes. Roll forward after introducing photos-only memberships: older releases normalize unknown roles to `member`, so rolling back before this role was added would widen access. Revoke photos-only memberships before any such rollback.
- **Privacy:** Read `docs/analytics.md` before changing analytics. PostHog is off without `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_KEY`. Public events go through a first-party endpoint with a signed HttpOnly visitor cookie (30 days) and session (30 minutes idle). Only approved public paths, campaign labels and referrer categories are accepted. Each canonical member has a random persistent analytics id, and each family has a separate random group id. Sign-in links the anonymous visit to the member; a visitor can belong to only one member. No names, emails, raw family ids, entry kinds, health details, chat text, log contents, private URLs or free-text properties may reach PostHog. No app pageviews, autocapture, replay, surveys or heatmaps. Exceptions retain approved classes, codes and static context, without raw messages or stack traces. Rate-limit buckets use keyed hashes, cleared after a day. Feedback links open an email to `CONTACT_EMAIL`; nothing is sent until the person sends it.
- **For mom:** Family 1 retains its existing care page; if it is absent, `getCarePerson()` creates an empty page with `DEFAULT_CARE_PERSON = "Mom"`. Existing care-person keys and records are retained. Other families opt in to a care page. Access goes through `careAccess`, and doses just save (only a likely duplicate within 30 minutes asks); a dose within her "Early doses" grace (`settings.early_grace_min`, 30 minutes by default, capped at an eighth of the gap, never for opioids (`EARLY_GRACE_EXACT_NAMES`), and not adding up past it over a day's doses in a row; `earlyGraceMin` in `care.ts`) gets no early note, and the 24-hour limit is always checked. Her data lives only in `maple_care_*` and never shows in Maple's views; every read goes through `careAccess`, which follows each person's level on her page (her own account always has all of it); no dose numbers anywhere by default; limits are in the label's own unit. Her next medicine rides on a 44px strip on top of Today's dock (one rule picks it, `pressingPlan`); after a Took it, that medicine holds its slot as "Logged 6:56 AM" for a minute and the strip's and Lantern's taps wait 1.5 s, so a double tap never logs a second medicine (her tab's fixed list takes two quick taps on two medicines). A name with extra words beyond a plan's ("Tylenol 3", "Tylenol PM") is another product and never counts toward that plan (`matchPlan`). Claude uses the saved care-person name (`copyForFamily` and `mapleInstructions`), distinguishes the baby from the care person and asks whose medicine it is before logging a medicine that's also on her list, counts a brand or generic name toward the medicine on her list (`SAME_MEDICINE` in `care-mcp.ts`, exact single-ingredient names only), and may copy a photographed label or discharge sheet into her list (`add_mom_medicines`, which needs `read_back_confirmed: true`) only after reading every value back and an explicit yes, never filling in a number that isn't on the paper and never adding a second plan for a medicine she has. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("For mom").
