# Maple

Maple's Log: the Brooker family's newborn tracker for **Maple Jane** (Matt and Brittany's daughter, due October 20, 2026). Split out of [Brooker-Fam/brooker-wedding](https://github.com/Brooker-Fam/brooker-wedding), where it lived at `brooker.family/maple`. Full design and ops notes: `docs/maple.md`.

**This repo is public.** Never commit secrets, `.env*` files, real log entries, screenshots of the signed-in app, or anything about Maple's or Brittany's health beyond what the code itself says.

## Stack

- **Next.js 16** (App Router), TypeScript, **Tailwind CSS v4**, Framer Motion
- **Neon Postgres** (`@neondatabase/serverless`), **Vercel Blob**, Web Push (`web-push`), MCP (`mcp-handler`)
- **pnpm** 11, Node 22+
- **Vercel** project `maple` in the `guava-tri` team. Pushing to `main` deploys production.

## Commands

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm dev               # http://localhost:3000 (the public site; the app is /maple)
pnpm build             # runs migrations, then next build
pnpm typecheck
pnpm lint
pnpm check:maple-feed  # the nursing engine's checks
pnpm check:maple-care  # Brittany's medicines' checks
pnpm check:maple-chat  # the in-app chat's checks
pnpm check:site        # the waitlist's checks
pnpm check:maple-growth   # the WHO growth tables and percentile math
pnpm check:maple-tenancy  # families kept apart, on an in-memory Postgres
pnpm check:maple-parity   # family 1's answers unchanged by the family code
pnpm check:email          # the Resend email helper and templates
pnpm check:analytics      # the allowed events and their privacy boundaries
```

## Hosting, origin and env

- The public origin comes from `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPLE_ORIGIN` (default `https://maplebaby.app`) via `src/lib/maple/origin.ts`. Use `MAPLE_ORIGIN` / `MAPLE_HOST` / `MCP_URL` from there; never hard-code a host.
- Production env (Vercel): `DATABASE_URL`, `DATABASE_URL_UNPOOLED`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, `ADMIN_USER`, `ADMIN_PASS`, `CRON_SECRET`, `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` (the connected Blob store), `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPLE_ORIGIN`. Optional: `MCP_SECRET`, `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`/`VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `QSTASH_*`, `MAPLE_TICK_NET_MIN`, `MAPLE_ALLOWED_EMAILS`, `MAPLE_BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_KEY`.
- Email (Resend, all environments): `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_EMAIL_DOMAIN` (`maplebaby.app`). Addressed invites send through `emailInvite` after the sender's daily cap; a newly created family's owner gets `emailWelcome`. Without the env vars, `sendEmail` (`src/lib/email/`) returns not-sent and invite callers show the link instead. Invite links expire after 7 days, but using or expiring one does not erase its address; revoking or replacing an unused invite deletes that row. See `docs/maple.md` ("Email").
- Session cookies, MCP tokens and VAPID keys are derived from `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` / `ADMIN_PASS` (or `MCP_SECRET`), which match brooker-wedding's. Maple-only `MAPLE_SESSION_SECRET` (with `MAPLE_SESSION_SECRET_PREV` holding the old `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) and `MCP_SECRET` (with `MCP_SECRET_PREV` holding whatever signed before) replace them; what only a `_PREV` key opens reaches family 1 only. Sign-up (`MAPLE_SIGNUP`) stays off until both are set. Pin `VAPID_*` before changing `MCP_SECRET`. Order: `docs/multi-family-plan.md` Phase 0.

## Database location (read before any schema change)

Maple uses its own Neon database. The original `maple_*` tables remain intact in brooker-wedding's database, protected by statement triggers that reject writes. Never remove those blockers or roll back to a deployment using the old database without reconciling writes made since the transfer. `upload_rate_limit` was excluded from the transfer; each app has its own table. Cutover and rollback details: `docs/multi-family-plan.md` 2.6.

`MAPLE_READ_ONLY=1` pauses every Maple API, OAuth, and waitlist request with a retryable 503; changing it requires a deployment. Complete preparation and verification of the transfer utility before enabling it. Keep the pause as short as possible because timer controls cannot queue offline.

- Migrations (`src/lib/migrate.ts`) stay additive and idempotent: `CREATE ... IF NOT EXISTS`, `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`. Never drop, rename or retype a column, and never add a `NOT NULL` column without a default: a Vercel instant rollback puts older code back on the migrated schema without a build. The contract steps are a separate script (plan Phase 3), never `migrate.ts`.
- Sends are claimed per phone in `maple_push_sent`, so a notice goes out once whichever deployment ticks. A claim without a family (only older code writes one) keeps sign-up off for 7 days (`olderCopyActive`).
- The family is in `America/New_York`; all "today"/clock logic goes through `src/lib/maple/time.ts`, never the server's zone.

## The public site

`/` (the home page), `/privacy`, `/terms` and `/waitlist` live in `src/app/(site)/`, with parts in `src/components/site/`, drawn in the app's `mp-*` tokens (`site.css`). The header and footer link to the app as "Family sign-in"; `/maple*` is `noindex` (meta and `X-Robots-Tag`) and stays crawlable so that can be read.

- **Waitlist:** `POST /api/waitlist` (`src/lib/waitlist.ts`, table `maple_waitlist`) keeps an email address and nothing else. It has its own hourly bucket per connection (`checkWaitlistRateLimit`), a daily cap on new addresses (`WAITLIST_DAILY_MAX`), refuses cross-site posts (`Sec-Fetch-Site`), answers 204 whether or not the address was already there, and sends a form posted before the script loaded to `/waitlist?r=<outcome>`. `pnpm check:site`.
- **Legal copy follows the code.** Any change to what Maple keeps, sends, shows on a lock screen or deletes needs the matching change to `/privacy` or `/terms`, and a new `LEGAL_VERSION` / `LEGAL_UPDATED` in `src/components/site/legal.tsx`. `CONTACT_EMAIL` there is the only deletion and waitlist-removal route, so it must reach a real inbox.

## The app

Private, collaborative newborn tracker: nursing L/R timers, bottles, pumping, diapers + stool colors, sleep, tummy time, temperature/vitals/skin checks, growth on WHO percentiles (the girls' or boys' standard by the baby's sex, the measurements alone when it isn't set), notes and doctor questions, photos and milestones. Full design and ops notes: `docs/maple.md`.

- **Access:** Google sign-in (identity only) + `maple_members` (roles owner / member / viewer). First Google sign-in claims owner unless `MAPLE_ALLOWED_EMAILS` is set. Fallback sign-in with `ADMIN_USER`/`ADMIN_PASS` as the "admin" member. One-time invite links for family without Google. Outside family 1, adding someone by Google address is a pending invite (`maple_invites.email`): they join only by its link, signed in as that address, and a membership nobody accepted (`accepted_at`) opens nothing. Google redirects to `{site}/api/maple/auth/callback`, where `site` is the origin the browser is on (`signInSite`: this app, or `https://brooker.family`, which forwards here); each must be an authorized redirect URI on the OAuth client (`GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`).
- **Data:** `maple_entries` (one row per event: `kind` + JSONB `data`, metric units; running timers have `active = TRUE`; soft deletes; `client_id` for idempotent retries). The family is in `America/New_York`; all "today"/clock logic goes through `src/lib/maple/time.ts`, never the server's zone.
- **Medical guidance** lives only in `src/lib/maple/guidelines.ts` (thresholds + exact copy, sourced from AAP/CDC/WHO/ABM). Alerts always say who to call; never suggest medicine doses. `insights.ts` applies the rules for both the app and the MCP tools. Warnings and reminders are off unless `settings.show_alerts` is true (Family → On Today → Warnings & reminders; the family doesn't log consistently enough for them), but readings are still checked as they're entered.
- **AI:** MCP server at `/api/maple/mcp` (Streamable HTTP, discrete tools in `src/lib/maple/mcp-tools.ts`). OAuth goes through the `/oauth/*` shim; connections (RFC 8707 `resource` = `{origin}/api/maple/mcp`) sign in with Google and get `maple:<email>` tokens, bound to that exact URL. Refresh tokens go only to codes redirected to Claude, ChatGPT or loopback (`trustedRedirect`); admin ones lapse after 90 idle days. Setup page: `/maple/connect`.
- **In-app chat (the Chat tab):** `POST /api/maple/chat` runs the MCP tools for the signed-in member (`source: "chat"`, "via Maple chat") with `zai/glm-5.3-flash` through AI Gateway (OIDC; ZDR and no-training on every request), from the Chat tab: one continuous conversation per person, no New chat, nobody else's visible. The model reads only its recent end (`chat/window.ts`: whole exchanges, at most 24 messages and about 12K tokens); GET reads it a page at a time (`chat/history.ts`), DELETE removes all of it. Each member accepts short terms first (`maple_consents`, `consent.ts`; the POST refuses without them), and her page (medicines, temperatures, questions for her OB) reaches the chat only once she has turned that on herself (`chat_care`; until then her tools are out, Maple's tools leave her out, `ctx.care`, and a thread that ever ran with it open rolls over, `care_open`). Requests route only to the price row's `providers` (ZDR, at or under its price). `delete_entry` and her `fix_mom_entry` delete wait for a tap (`undo_last` and edits, hers included, don't); viewers get reads only, decided on the server, and open only chats that ran as a viewer; her medicine-list tools are left out; the crisis help card shows even when a message is turned away. Caps fail closed; usage is token counts and micro-dollar BigInt cost only, never content. Optional env: `MAPLE_CHAT_MODEL` (must be priced in `chat/prices.ts`), `MAPLE_CHAT_DAILY_BUDGET_MICRO`, `MAPLE_CHAT_MONTHLY_BUDGET_MICRO` (family 1's), `MAPLE_CHAT_GLOBAL_MONTHLY_MICRO` (every other family together, default 25000000; `chatBudget`). An operator pauses one family's chat with `maple_families.settings.chat_paused = true`. `pnpm check:maple-chat`. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("In-app chat").
- **Hands-free:** `POST /api/maple/quick` with a personal token (Family → Shortcuts & assistants) for iOS Shortcuts / Siri. Her `mom_dose` returns `next_ok_in_minutes` for a one-action Start Timer; "Maple status" includes her medicines for anyone who can read them. `back_and_forth` (with a feed running) goes both sides from now; `switch` while she's going both says there's nothing to switch.
- **Notifications:** Web Push, opt-in per phone (iPhone needs the Home Screen app): her medicines (OK again, set times, "Logged" that closes the loop on the other phones, never a dose or "take it now") and an opt-in nursing timer on the lock screen; nothing else notifies. `src/lib/maple/push.ts` sends (claimed once per phone, retried on failure, 3 h cutoff), `push-wake.ts` decides when (`dueMoments`) and is the clock: Upstash QStash wake-ups at the exact moments plus a safety-net schedule (`MAPLE_TICK_NET_MIN`, default 30), published from writes (`push-hooks.ts`, via `after()` in the stores, so the app, Siri and Claude all count) and scheduled ticks, never from polls. Without `QSTASH_*` env vars, `.github/workflows/maple-reminders.yml` (every 5 min, when GitHub runs it, and only once the repo variable `MAPLE_TICK_URL` is set) and open apps' polls drive `/api/maple/push/tick`; with them, the tick ignores GitHub runs away from :04/:34. The app sends a per-install `X-Maple-Device` id (`device.ts`) only so "Logged" skips the phone that did it; no person is ever named. The app's side is `src/components/maple/notify/`: one provider (mounted in `MapleApp`) owns this phone's state and the one Notifications sheet (`useNotifications().open()`, `NotificationsRow`), re-checks the subscription on each open (a dropped one reads "Stopped" with a one-tap Turn on), offers "Remind me at …" in a dose toast on a phone that's off (at most 3 times), and sign-out turns the phone off. VAPID keys derive from `MCP_SECRET` (else `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) unless `VAPID_*` are set; changing that secret makes every phone turn notifications on again. Family setup steps and copy: `docs/maple.md` → Notifications.
- **Tabs:** Today, Brittany (her page; hidden for view-only guests), History (Timeline | Patterns behind one pinned switch), Moments, Chat (a full page, in every state, viewers too; mounted once visited, so its draft and a reply still coming survive a tab switch), Family (settings only). A family still setting up (`onboarding/`) sees only its setup, as the Chat page in the Chat tab's frame (`chat/page.ts`). Which tab shows lives in `AppNav.tsx` (`useAppNav().go()`; "log"/"patterns" open History on that segment); `useCareNav()` opens her tab and the shared sheets: Help (`HelpSheet.tsx`, every "who do we call"), For the doctor (`DoctorSheet.tsx`: questions, visit summary, her doctor's numbers), her temperature, the picker and the Dose sheet. Today keeps one order in every state (the hero folds while a timer runs; "While you slept" is one line). More is headed by person. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("Where things live").
- **Dock:** Today's logging buttons (`Dock.tsx`, with her strip on top); every timer action lives there and in the Lantern, with `useTimerActions.tsx` saying what each tap did (Undo puts back exactly what the server had). Going back and forth (a `"B"` part: both sides, not tracked) is offered from a feed's second Switch, on a start within 30 minutes of the last feed, and in the card's … menu; then Switch's place only asks "Which side is she on now?" and saves nothing until answered. Family → On Today has log-wide Bottles and Pumping switches (`settings.feeds_bottle` / `track_pump`, on unless switched off; `logSwitches` in `MoreSheet.tsx`): off hides the Bottle tile (Left and Right take its room) or Pump in More, never anything logged, and Claude and Siri can still log either. Feeds are counted from the log, never stored (`nursingRuns`, `feedClusters`, `lastFedAnchor`, `nextSideFor` in `feed.ts`, used by Today, History, Patterns, Claude and Siri alike): under 10 minutes off the breast is the same feed whether Pause or Done was tapped, 3+ feeds each starting under 30 minutes after the last ended are a cluster (each feed counts), and Last fed after a long back-and-forth feed counts from 40 minutes before it ended. The cluster copy and its sources live in `guidelines.ts`; there's no cluster alert. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("The nursing screen", "Medical guidance", "How it works").
- **Look:** its own calm, native-feeling skin, not the site's storybook one. Use the semantic `mp-*` colors (values in `src/app/maple/maple.css` cover day, dark and the night look, so no `dark:` variants), icons from `src/components/maple/icons.tsx`, and the kit in `ui.tsx`. No emoji in the app's chrome (members' avatar emoji and family-typed text are fine). Details in `docs/maple.md`.
- **Photos:** Vercel Blob under `maple/`, private store via `MAPLE_BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` (falls back to the public `brooker-wedding-photos` store via `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`); always served through the auth proxy `/api/maple/photo`, never by blob URL.
- **Privacy:** PostHog is off without `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_KEY`. Public-site pageviews (allowlisted paths, blog articles grouped) use memory-only ids; no analytics cookies, persistent analytics identifiers, query strings, referrers, page titles or browser/device properties. `signup_started` is server-only, with a fresh random id after an accepted new-family sign-up submission. `signup_completed` and app events are server-only, under an HMAC of the family id, never a name or email. These ids are not linked to the browser id. App events are only `signed_up`, `onboarding_completed`, `invite_sent`, `chat_used`, `entry_logged` (new baby-log entries only) and `app_opened`. No entry kind, health details, chat text or log contents may reach analytics. No app pageviews, autocapture, replay, surveys, heatmaps or person profiles. `captureServerException` sends only an error's class, code and stack frames (`scrubError`), never its message or entry contents; `errorResponse` logs the same scrubbed error. Rate-limit buckets use keyed hashes of the IP or invite sender, cleared after a day. Feedback links open an email to `CONTACT_EMAIL`; nothing is sent until the person sends it.
- **For mom:** Her page works like Maple's log: it always exists (no setup), anyone signed in except view-only guests can use it, and doses just save (only a likely duplicate within 30 minutes asks); a dose within her "Early doses" grace (`settings.early_grace_min`, 30 minutes by default, capped at an eighth of the gap, never for opioids (`EARLY_GRACE_EXACT_NAMES`), and not adding up past it over a day's doses in a row; `earlyGraceMin` in `care.ts`) gets no early note, and the 24-hour limit is always checked. Her data lives only in `maple_care_*` and never shows in Maple's views; every read goes through `careAccess`; no dose numbers anywhere by default; limits are in the label's own unit. Her next medicine rides on a 44px strip on top of Today's dock (one rule picks it, `pressingPlan`); after a Took it, that medicine holds its slot as "Logged 6:56 AM" for a minute and the strip's and Lantern's taps wait 1.5 s, so a double tap never logs a second medicine (her tab's fixed list takes two quick taps on two medicines). A name with extra words beyond a plan's ("Tylenol 3", "Tylenol PM") is another product and never counts toward that plan (`matchPlan`). Claude knows her by name (`MAPLE_INSTRUCTIONS`), takes "she" as Maple and asks "Maple's or Brittany's?" before logging a medicine that's also on her list, counts a brand or generic name toward the medicine on her list (`SAME_MEDICINE` in `care-mcp.ts`, exact single-ingredient names only), and may copy a photographed label or discharge sheet into her list (`add_mom_medicines`, which needs `read_back_confirmed: true`) only after reading every value back and an explicit yes, never filling in a number that isn't on the paper and never adding a second plan for a medicine she has. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("For mom").
