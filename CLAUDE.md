# Maple

Maple's Log is a private, collaborative newborn tracker. Split out of [Brooker-Fam/brooker-wedding](https://github.com/Brooker-Fam/brooker-wedding), where it lived at `brooker.family/maple`. Full design and ops notes: `docs/maple.md`.

**This repo is public.** Never commit secrets, `.env*` files, real log entries, screenshots of the signed-in app, or personal family or health details. Use generic examples in shared app copy and documentation.

## Stack

- **Next.js 16** (App Router), TypeScript, **Tailwind CSS v4**, Framer Motion
- **Neon Postgres** (`@neondatabase/serverless`), **Vercel Blob**, Web Push (`web-push`), MCP (`mcp-handler`)
- **pnpm** 11, Node 22+
- **Vercel** project `maple` in the `guava-tri` team. Pushing to `main` deploys production.

## Hosting, origin and env

- The public origin comes from `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPLE_ORIGIN` (default `https://maplebaby.app`) via `src/lib/maple/origin.ts`. Use `MAPLE_ORIGIN` / `MAPLE_HOST` / `MCP_URL` from there; never hard-code a host.
- Production env (Vercel): `DATABASE_URL`, `DATABASE_URL_UNPOOLED`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, `ADMIN_USER`, `ADMIN_PASS`, `CRON_SECRET`, `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` (the connected Blob store), `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPLE_ORIGIN`. Optional: `MCP_SECRET`, `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`/`VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `QSTASH_*`, `MAPLE_TICK_NET_MIN`, `MAPLE_ALLOWED_EMAILS`, `MAPLE_BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_POSTHOG_KEY`.
- Additional sign-in env: email uses `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_EMAIL_DOMAIN` and the session key; optional `MAPLE_EMAIL_AUTH_DAILY_MAX` defaults to 1,000. Apple uses `APPLE_CLIENT_ID` (Services ID), `APPLE_TEAM_ID`, `APPLE_KEY_ID`, `APPLE_PRIVATE_KEY` (P-256 `.p8` PEM). New methods stay hidden until configured. Apple setup, callback and relay registration: `docs/auth.md`.
- Email (Resend, all environments): `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_EMAIL_DOMAIN` (`maplebaby.app`). Addressed invites send through `emailInvite` after the sender's daily cap; a newly created family's owner gets `emailWelcome`. Without the env vars, `sendEmail` (`src/lib/email/`) returns not-sent and invite callers show the link instead. Invite links expire after 7 days, but using or expiring one does not erase its address; revoking or replacing an unused invite deletes that row. See `docs/maple.md` ("Email").
- Session cookies, MCP tokens and VAPID keys are derived from `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` / `ADMIN_PASS` (or `MCP_SECRET`), which match brooker-wedding's. Maple-only `MAPLE_SESSION_SECRET` (with `MAPLE_SESSION_SECRET_PREV` holding the old `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) and `MCP_SECRET` (with `MCP_SECRET_PREV` holding whatever signed before) replace them; what only a `_PREV` key opens reaches family 1 only. Sign-up (`MAPLE_SIGNUP`) stays off until both are set. Pin `VAPID_*` before changing `MCP_SECRET`. Order: `docs/multi-family-plan.md` Phase 0.

## Database location (read before any schema change)

Maple uses its own Neon database. The original `maple_*` tables remain intact in brooker-wedding's database, protected by statement triggers that reject writes. Never remove those blockers or roll back to a deployment using the old database without reconciling writes made since the transfer. `upload_rate_limit` was excluded from the transfer; each app has its own table. Cutover and rollback details: `docs/multi-family-plan.md` 2.6.

`MAPLE_READ_ONLY=1` pauses every Maple API, OAuth, and waitlist request with a retryable 503; changing it requires a deployment. Complete preparation and verification of the transfer utility before enabling it. Keep the pause as short as possible because timer controls cannot queue offline.

- Migrations (`src/lib/migrate.ts`) stay additive and idempotent: `CREATE ... IF NOT EXISTS`, `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`. Never drop, rename or retype a column, and never add a `NOT NULL` column without a default: a Vercel instant rollback puts older code back on the migrated schema without a build. The contract steps are a separate script (plan Phase 3), never `migrate.ts`.
- Sends are claimed per phone in `maple_push_sent`, so a notice goes out once whichever deployment ticks. A recent legacy claim without a family keeps sign-up off (`olderCopyActive`). Its timestamp is not a reliable writer timestamp: wake claims use scheduled delivery times, and retries can refresh existing rows. Attribute copied claims to family 1 only after verifying the isolated database and the old database’s durable write blockers.
- All "today"/clock logic goes through `src/lib/maple/time.ts`, using each family's saved timezone (legacy default `America/New_York`), never the server's zone.

## The public site

`/` (the home page), `/privacy`, `/terms` and `/waitlist` live in `src/app/(site)/`, with parts in `src/components/site/`, drawn in the app's `mp-*` tokens (`site.css`). The header and footer link to the app as "Family sign-in"; `/maple*` is `noindex` (meta and `X-Robots-Tag`) and stays crawlable so that can be read.

- **Waitlist:** `POST /api/waitlist` (`src/lib/waitlist.ts`, table `maple_waitlist`) keeps an email address and nothing else. It has its own hourly bucket per connection (`checkWaitlistRateLimit`), a daily cap on new addresses (`WAITLIST_DAILY_MAX`), refuses cross-site posts (`Sec-Fetch-Site`), answers 204 whether or not the address was already there, and sends a form posted before the script loaded to `/waitlist?r=<outcome>`. `pnpm check:site`.
- **Legal copy follows the code.** Any change to what Maple keeps, sends, shows on a lock screen or deletes needs the matching change to `/privacy` or `/terms`, and a new `LEGAL_VERSION` / `LEGAL_UPDATED` in `src/components/site/legal.tsx`. `CONTACT_EMAIL` there is the only deletion and waitlist-removal route, so it must reach a real inbox.

## The app

Private, collaborative newborn tracker: nursing L/R timers, bottles, pumping, diapers + stool colors, sleep, tummy time, temperature/vitals/skin checks, growth on WHO percentiles (the girls' or boys' standard by the baby's sex, the measurements alone when it isn't set), notes and doctor questions, photos and milestones. Full design and ops notes: `docs/maple.md`.

- **Names and milestones:** Every family, including family 1, uses its saved baby name/nickname, sex-derived pronouns and care-person name in the app, Siri, MCP and chat. With no profile, copy falls back to "the baby" and "Mom". All families get the same neutral milestone presets. Older personal milestone ids have neutral display labels for compatibility, but are never offered for new moments. This copy cleanup does not rewrite saved entries, profile values or care-person keys.

- **Access:** Google, email codes and Apple verify identity; `maple_members` grants access (roles owner / member / viewer / photos), plus each person's level on her page (`care_access`: none / view / log, set by an owner apart from the role; `src/lib/maple/access.ts`, enforced only through `careAccess`; NULL is the role's default). Owners manage people in Settings → People & permissions, from the chat, and over MCP (`people-mcp.ts` over `people.ts`: `list_people`, and for owners `invite_person`, `change_access`, `remove_person`, `cancel_invite`); over MCP the three changes need `confirmed: true` after a read-back, and in the chat each waits for a tap on its card. Owners can make an existing member an owner (Settings Manage → Make owner, or `change_access` access `owner`); it clears their `care_access`, and since an owner can't change or remove another owner, it can't be undone in the app. Older releases ignore `care_access` and give every member all of her page, so roll forward: before any rollback past it, make restricted members viewers (`UPDATE maple_members SET role = 'viewer' WHERE role = 'member' AND care_access IS NOT NULL`), which older releases read as no page and no logging. `maple_auth_identities` maps verified email addresses and Apple subjects to the existing member key, preserving family access, removals and log attribution. Family settings explicitly connects another method; never merge separate accounts/logs automatically. First Google sign-in claims owner unless `MAPLE_ALLOWED_EMAILS` is set. Fallback sign-in with `ADMIN_USER`/`ADMIN_PASS` as the "admin" member. One-time invite links remain available. Outside family 1, adding someone by email is a pending invite (`maple_invites.email`): they join only by its link, signed in as that address, and a membership nobody accepted (`accepted_at`) opens nothing. Google redirects to `{site}/api/maple/auth/callback`, where `site` is the origin the browser is on (`signInSite`: this app, or `https://brooker.family`, which forwards here); each must be an authorized redirect URI on the OAuth client (`GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`). Apple uses `/api/maple/auth/apple/callback` with its own state/nonce validation. Email challenges expire in ten minutes, are single use and store HMACs plus encrypted context. Setup and checks: `docs/auth.md`.
- **Data:** `maple_entries` (one row per event: `kind` + JSONB `data`, metric units; running timers have `active = TRUE`; soft deletes; `client_id` for idempotent retries). All "today"/clock logic goes through `src/lib/maple/time.ts`, using each family's saved timezone (legacy default `America/New_York`), never the server's zone.
- **Medical guidance** lives in `src/lib/maple/guidelines.ts` (thresholds + exact copy) and `src/lib/maple/guidelines/development.ts` (sourced discoveries, each written for its age stage, and milestones), sourced from AAP/CDC/WHO/ABM and the sources linked by each discovery. Alerts always say who to call; never suggest medicine doses. `insights.ts` applies the rules for both the app and the MCP tools. Warnings and reminders are off unless `settings.show_alerts` is true (Settings → On Today → Warnings & reminders; incomplete logging can make count-based alerts misleading), but readings are still checked as they're entered.
- **AI:** MCP server at `/api/maple/mcp` (Streamable HTTP, discrete tools in `src/lib/maple/mcp-tools.ts`). OAuth goes through the `/oauth/*` shim; connections (RFC 8707 `resource` = `{origin}/api/maple/mcp`) sign in with Google and get `maple:<email>` tokens, bound to that exact URL. Refresh tokens go only to codes redirected to Claude, ChatGPT, Muse's or Grok's exact callback, or loopback (`trustedRedirect`); admin ones lapse after 90 idle days. Setup page: `/maple/connect`.
- **In-app chat (the Chat tab):** Photos use the private, member-scoped `/api/maple/chat/images` upload/read route and Neon storage, separately from Moments. Up to eight images per message; original files are resized and stripped of metadata. Chat consent version 2 covers photos, and deleting history removes them.  `POST /api/maple/chat` runs the MCP tools for the signed-in member (`source: "chat"`, "via Maple chat") with `zai/glm-5.3-flash` through AI Gateway (OIDC; ZDR and no-training on every request), from the Chat tab: one continuous conversation per person, no New chat, nobody else's visible. The model reads only its recent end (`chat/window.ts`: whole exchanges, at most 24 messages and about 12K tokens); GET reads it a page at a time (`chat/history.ts`), DELETE removes all of it. Each step sends the core tools plus only the groups the turn needs (`chat/tool-groups.ts`: every chat tool mapped once; groups come from the newest message and its [App] lines, the window's tool calls and older messages, `load_tools`, or a call to a tool not sent, which loads its group for the next step), kept under `TOOL_CEILING` by dropping the least recently used group, while MCP connections still get every tool. Each member accepts short terms first (`maple_consents`, `consent.ts`; the POST refuses without them), and her page (medicines, temperatures, questions for her OB) reaches the chat only once she has turned that on herself (`chat_care`; until then her tools are out, Maple's tools leave her out, `ctx.care`, and a thread that ever ran with it open rolls over, `care_open`), and then only for owners and members whose level lets them read it (`chatCareOpen`, never a viewer: "view" brings her reads only; someone set to "none" opens only threads that never had it open, `threadAccess`). Requests route only to the price row's `providers` (ZDR, at or under its price). `delete_entry`, her `fix_mom_entry` delete and the people changes (`invite_person`, `change_access`, `remove_person`, owners only) wait for a tap (`undo_last` and edits, hers included, don't); viewers get reads only, decided on the server, and open only chats that ran as a viewer; her medicine-list tools are left out; the crisis help card shows even when a message is turned away. `web_search` (`chat/web-search.ts`) sends only a short phrase, with the family's names, emails, phone numbers and links taken out on the server, through AI Gateway to Perplexity (outside ZDR) in a request of its own that sees nothing else; health questions keep to `HEALTH_SITES`, two searches a message, its fee counted in the budgets, and the card shows the exact phrase (chat consent version 3). Caps fail closed; usage is token counts and micro-dollar BigInt cost only, never content. Optional env: `MAPLE_CHAT_MODEL` (must be priced in `chat/prices.ts`), `MAPLE_CHAT_DAILY_BUDGET_MICRO`, `MAPLE_CHAT_MONTHLY_BUDGET_MICRO` (family 1's), `MAPLE_CHAT_GLOBAL_MONTHLY_MICRO` (every other family together, default 25000000; `chatBudget`). An operator pauses one family's chat with `maple_families.settings.chat_paused = true`. `pnpm check:maple-chat`. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("In-app chat").
- **Family memories:** what a family asked to have kept in mind (feeding plan, health context they reported, sleep setup, routines, care team, how they like answers), one record per fact with who said it and where (`src/lib/maple/memory/`, modeled on Moku's athlete memories). Shared by the family: everyone but photos-only guests reads them in Settings → Memories; owners and members add, edit, resolve, archive and forget them. The chat reads them as `[App]` lines on the newest message each turn (never stored, never in the instructions) and saves in the turn they're said; connected apps get the same five tools and `family_memories` in `maple_status`. Edits name the memory's revision; the turn's `memoryEpoch` refuses its writes after a Forget or a switch change. Never her page's things. Forget deletes every version and keeps older chat messages (everyone's) out of the model. Switches: "Let chats save memories", "Use memories in chats". Details in `docs/maple.md` ("Family memories").
- **Sleep plan:** a gentle move from held sleep to the bassinet, never crying it out (`src/lib/maple/sleep/`, guidance in `guidelines/sleep.ts`, `/api/maple/sleep`, the app's side in `components/maple/sleep/`). Own tables (`maple_sleep_plans`, `_tries`, `_shifts`), so older releases ignore them. A bassinet try sits on one sleep entry (running, overlapping, or one it makes, `made_sleep`) and never counts as sleep itself; finishing stops that sleep except "Picked up" (still asleep). The try day runs noon to noon in the family's timezone; try numbers, retries and age are worked out from the log and birth date, never stored or hard-coded. Stages 1–4 change only when the family says so: Maple shows when recent tries could support the next (`SUGGEST`, its own rule), with Not now. Holding turns track who holds her awake and the planned handoff, never planned inside the next holder's protected window on the roster (`sleep/handoff.ts`: its end, or none) (a start can log a sleep in arms, an ordinary sleep entry that a try sits on, that a feed starting or ending the turn awake stops, and that the turn's card shows); "I'm getting sleepy", the holder's only, offers exactly two things, the bassinet now or waking the partner, which is only an ask (`asked_to`, `asked_at`; Handoff now; Siri's `handoff` from the holder too): nothing changes hands until they tap "I've got her" or the holder taps "Alex has her", and ten minutes on the holder sees the two choices again. Nothing presents sleeping with a baby on an adult as safe. Guidance items carry age windows, sources, a reviewed date and `needsReview` (`pendingReview()`); during a nursing feed from 10 PM to 6 AM the feed card says quietly to wake someone to sit with you, or lay her in the bassinet (`nightFeedLine`, NICHD; no push); progress shows counts with sample sizes, no scores or streaks, and patterns only as "Early observation" from 10 tries. Sleep goals (`sleep/goals.ts`, `maple_sleep_goals`): a target date, targets counted from the log with what each count is out of, weekly steps and a night roster of protected windows, set with the chat's coach (`sleep_goal`, saved only after a read-back and a yes) or by hand in the plan sheet's Goals tab (`SleepGoals.tsx`, `GoalForm.tsx`); an edit names the goal's revision, and a stale one is a 409 shown as it is now. A roster window may say how her feeds in it are covered (`covers`: nursing, a bottle of expressed milk, none planned). For a family going back to work, the empty Goals tab and the chat's first goal turn ask five questions (`backToWorkGoal`, `sleep_goal` add with `back_to_work`) and read back one goal, a roster and three steps timed from the day back, saved on a yes with no logs needed; the goal keeps `template` (who goes back and when, who nurses) for its coach notes. Check-ins (`maple_sleep_checkins`, `SleepCheckin.tsx`): each adult says the morning after whether their own protected window held (yes, partly, not this time, an optional note), saved only by them; Today asks the adults on last night's roster, never by notification. Owners and members read every check-in; a view-only guest never sees one (not in the state, its change token, the route, the tools or the chat), and photos-only guests see no sleep at all. Tools: `sleep_plan`, `set_sleep_plan`, `bassinet_try`, `holding`, `sleep_goal`; refs `t12` / `h7` / `g3`. Families with no sleep rows keep their state and change token as before. Never store the care person's health here. Every goal text and the plan's goal refuse crying it out by any name and the baby asleep anywhere but on her back in her own bare, flat sleep space (on or with an adult, an adult bed, a couch or chair, a car seat or other sitting device, products not made for infant sleep, tummy or side, soft things, tilts, weights; a few Spanish, French and German phrasings) (`unsafeSleep`, in `cleanGoalInput` / `cleanPlanPatch`, so the tool, route and form all refuse, the form on Save): a backstop, not a guarantee. Steps away, held sleep while an adult says they're awake and the transfer pass, never on a couch, chair or adult bed; each refusal says what to try and its suggestion passes. Feeding words save, with a read-back asking for their clinician's yes; age, covered-feeds and window cautions (`goalCautions`) are warnings, never refusals. Counts are only what was logged or answered in the morning (`nightLog`), never "no feeds" for a night nobody logged; under 3 such nights a week is "too few to tell" (`ENOUGH_NIGHTS`), 7 empty nights are said once and never asked about, and only the family restarts or eases off a goal (`counts_from`, `paused_until`, `SleepReturn.tsx`); a review day ends Last night with the look-back (`SleepReview.tsx`). `sleep_plan` answers compact with goals, most urgent first (`view`: goals, guidance, all; `goal` for one), and stays under the chat's cut by naming any goal it leaves out (`ALL_VIEW_MAX`). Today's Last night card (`LastNight.tsx`, from 5 AM, no push) carries the check-in ("Did you get your 10 PM–2 AM sleep?", never "hold"), the night's gaps and nullable `place` / `untimed_try` / `handoff` answers on `maple_sleep_checkins`. Details in `docs/maple.md` ("Sleep plan").
- **Hands-free:** `POST /api/maple/quick` with a personal token (Avatar → Settings → Siri & assistants) for iOS Shortcuts / Siri. Her `mom_dose` returns `next_ok_in_minutes` for a one-action Start Timer; "Maple status" includes her medicines for anyone who can read them. `back_and_forth` (with a feed running) goes both sides from now; `switch` while she's going both says there's nothing to switch. The sleep plan rides it too (`quick/sleep.ts`, the app's own store calls): `put_down`, `outcome`, `asleep_in_arms`, `handoff` (the holder's asks; anyone else's takes over, with the catch-up from `sleep/catchup.ts`; with no turn, the speaker's starts asked), `checkin` (the speaker's own), `minutes_ago` (0–240, the family's zone) on `sleep`, `wake` and `put_down`, and `undo` (the speaker's newest Siri action in 30 minutes, with the app's Undo); "Maple status" adds one sleep line (a running try, the holding turn, or their check-in when due). `recap` ("what did I miss?") only reads, never moving `last_seen_at`: since their protected window began, else their last visit. Back Tap, the Action Button and NFC run a shortcut (Siri & assistants → Sleep plan shortcuts). `pnpm check:maple-tenancy quick-sleep`.
- **Notifications:** Web Push, opt-in per phone (iPhone needs the Home Screen app): her medicines (OK again, set times, "Logged" that closes the loop on the other phones, never a dose or "take it now"), an opt-in nursing timer on the lock screen, and opt-in handoff reminders for a holding turn (`prefs.handoff`: the planned handoff to the holder and next holder, "Handoff now" to the person asked; no names, nothing about her sleep); nothing else notifies. A roster adult's phone holds nursing and medicine notices in their own protected window unless they hold her or used Maple in the last 15 minutes (`push-quiet.ts`, `prefs.quiet_window`; never her own reminders on her phone, nor a handoff; a reminder still due goes at the window's end). `src/lib/maple/push.ts` sends (claimed once per phone, retried on failure, 3 h cutoff), `push-wake.ts` decides when (`dueMoments`) and is the clock: Upstash QStash wake-ups at the exact moments plus a safety-net schedule (`MAPLE_TICK_NET_MIN`, default 30), published from writes (`push-hooks.ts`, via `after()` in the stores) and scheduled ticks, never from polls. Without `QSTASH_*` env vars, `.github/workflows/maple-reminders.yml` (every 5 min, once the repo variable `MAPLE_TICK_URL` is set) and open apps' polls drive `/api/maple/push/tick`; with them, the tick ignores GitHub runs away from :04/:34. The app sends a per-install `X-Maple-Device` id (`device.ts`) only so "Logged" skips the phone that did it; no person is ever named. The app's side is `src/components/maple/notify/`: one provider (mounted in `MapleApp`) owns this phone's state and the one Notifications sheet (`useNotifications().open()`, `NotificationsRow`), re-checks the subscription on each open ("Stopped" offers a one-tap Turn on), offers "Remind me at …" in a dose toast on a phone that's off (at most 3 times), and sign-out turns the phone off. VAPID keys derive from `MCP_SECRET` (else `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) unless `VAPID_*` are set; changing that secret makes every phone turn notifications on again. Family setup steps and copy: `docs/maple.md` → Notifications.

Only approved public paths, campaign labels and referrer categories are accepted. Each canonical member has a random persistent analytics id, and each family has a separate random group id. Sign-in links the anonymous visit to the member; a visitor can belong to only one member. No names, emails, raw family ids, entry kinds, health details, chat text, log contents, private URLs or free-text properties may reach PostHog. No app pageviews, autocapture, replay, surveys or heatmaps. Exceptions retain approved classes, codes and static context, without raw messages or stack traces. Rate-limit buckets use keyed hashes, cleared after a day. Feedback links open an email to `CONTACT_EMAIL`; nothing is sent until the person sends it.
